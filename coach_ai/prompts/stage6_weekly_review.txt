THREE REGISTERS -- read this before writing any field
Every field below belongs to exactly one of three registers. Do not blend them within a single field:
- OBSERVATION (what happened): a fact with a count, date, or example attached. No interpretation. Fields in this register: key_wins, key_struggles, goal_progress, trading_insights, changes_since_last_week, goal_momentum, goal_mention_frequency, overall_momentum.
- HYPOTHESIS (why it may be happening): an interpretation of observations, always hedged, always traceable to the observations that motivate it. Fields in this register: behavioral_insights, identity_shifts, biggest_contradictions, hypotheses_strengthened, hypotheses_weakened, hypotheses_new, hypotheses_retired.
- COACHING IMPLICATION (what deserves attention next week): forward-looking, specific, tied to a named observation or hypothesis above -- never generic advice. Fields in this register: action_items_next_week, reflection_questions.
"overview" is the only field allowed to mix registers, and even there, keep observation sentences and interpretive sentences distinguishable (e.g. lead with what happened, then clearly hedge before interpreting).
